# Private Alpha Test Guide

## Purpose

This private alpha tests whether TrendIQ's core product loop helps a consumer
decide whether the controlled Ray-Ban Meta product seems worth considering
based on supported evidence. It is limited to exactly three invited users. It
does not test market size, pricing, retention, or public-scale operation.

## Participant instruction

Use TrendIQ to help you decide whether the product shown seems worth
considering. Explore it as you normally would, and tell me when you feel you
understand the result.

## Session procedure

1. Give the participant private access and the instruction above. Do not
   explain the interface.
2. Observe without coaching. If the participant is genuinely stuck, ask only,
   "What would you do next?" and record that intervention.
3. Allow the participant to find the product and Analyze action independently.
4. Observe how the participant interprets the analyzing or pending state.
5. After the result appears, ask the participant to explain the result and its
   confidence in their own words.
6. Observe whether supported, limited, and unavailable evidence are
   distinguished correctly.
7. Do not manufacture a failure or encourage an unnecessary Retry. If Retry
   appears naturally, ask what the participant expects it to do before they
   use it.
8. Complete the interview and record feedback immediately after the session.

Run the three sessions one at a time. Schedule each new analysis sufficiently
outside the 15-minute same-product deduplication window so each participant
experiences a real analysis lifecycle. Do not restart the process between
users merely to bypass deduplication.

## Observer checklist

Record Yes, Partly, or No and a short note for each item:

- Product purpose understood
- Analyze found without coaching
- Pending state understood
- Recommendation understood
- Confidence interpreted as evidence quality rather than certainty
- Evidence limits understood
- Watch Out noticed
- Missing evidence noticed
- Technical-language confusion observed
- Content appeared misleading or overly certain
- Trust or distrust reaction and reason
- Missing capability identified
- Desire to analyze another product
- Coaching required
- Bug, failure, or unreasonable delay

## Neutral interview questions

1. What do you think TrendIQ is intended to help with?
2. Before choosing Analyze, what did you expect would happen?
3. How would you describe the result in your own words?
4. What did the confidence information mean to you?
5. Which evidence seemed strongest or weakest?
6. What, if anything, affected your view of the product?
7. Was anything difficult to understand or unnecessary?
8. Did anything feel more certain or complete than it should?
9. What information did you expect but not find?
10. If other products were available, what would you want to analyze and why?
11. In what real situation, if any, would you use TrendIQ?
12. What, if anything, would make you return?

## Qualitative session outcomes

Do not convert three sessions into statistical thresholds.

### Strong signal

The participant completes the loop without meaningful coaching, explains the
result and confidence correctly, understands evidence limits, finds the result
useful for purchase thinking, and expresses a natural next use.

### Mixed signal

The core purpose and result are understood, but minor wording, hierarchy, or
evidence questions need clarification. Nothing materially misleads the user.

### Weak signal

The participant needs substantial coaching, misinterprets important parts,
finds little purchasing value, or sees no reason to use TrendIQ again, without
encountering a deceptive or unsafe result.

### Blocking product problem

The participant fundamentally misreads the recommendation or confidence
because of the presentation, perceives unsupported claims as facts, cannot
distinguish limited evidence from a supported conclusion, or finds the
correctly understood result irrelevant to a purchase decision.

- Result interpretation

personal information.
